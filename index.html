Peer Evaluation System

To maintain high standards, each weekend after the weekly meeting, every team member submits objective peer evaluations on four benchmarks: Deliverables, Commitment, Cooperation, and Initiative.

Weekly score = average of ratings submitted by six peers. Records are archived for leadership decisions, milestones, and responsibilities.
